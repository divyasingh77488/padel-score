# Padel Battle – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 October 2026_

Padel Battle is a padel scorekeeper for iPhone and Apple Watch. It does not collect, share or
sell any personal data.

- **No accounts, no servers.** Padel Battle has no sign-in and sends nothing to us or anyone
  else. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.
- **Matches stay on your devices.** Team names, scores and match history are stored only on
  your iPhone and Apple Watch. Matches move between your own iPhone and Apple Watch through
  Apple's watch connection, not through the internet.
- **Calendar (optional).** If you allow it, Padel Battle reads your calendar on your iPhone to
  find upcoming padel bookings. The calendar is read on the phone only. A booking's title,
  place and time are saved only when you add it to a match, and only on your devices.
  You can turn calendar access off at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security →
  Calendars**.
- **Deleting your data.** Delete matches in **History**, or delete the app to remove
  everything.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
