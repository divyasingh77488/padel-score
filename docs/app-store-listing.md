## Description (4000)

Padel Battle keeps the score of your padel match, so you can keep your mind on the game.

SET UP ON YOUR IPHONE
Enter the team names, who serves first and how 40–40 is played, then send the match to your
Apple Watch. Padel Battle can find your next court booking in your calendar and add the club
and time to the match.

SCORE ON YOUR WRIST
Tap the top or bottom half of the watch screen to give that team the point. Padel Battle
counts points, games and sets, shows who serves, and plays the tiebreak at 6–6. Made a
mis-tap? Undo takes back the last point.

PLAY BY YOUR RULES
Choose what happens at 40–40:
• Advantage – win two points in a row
• Golden point – the next point wins
• Star point – advantage twice, then a deciding point (the FIP rule)

COURT TIME OVER? NO PROBLEM
End a match whenever you like, finished or not, and sync the score to your iPhone's history.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your matches stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

No watch with you? Score the match on your iPhone instead.

## App Review notes

No sign-in is needed. To test: on the iPhone tap New match, then Send to watch; on the Apple
Watch open Padel Battle and tap the match. Calendar access is optional and only used to list
upcoming padel bookings on the New match screen.
